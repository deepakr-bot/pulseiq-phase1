# 5 questions a CEO asks about people

1. How many people work here now, and how many joined or left this month?
   -> KPI cards (headcount, hires, exits)
2. Is headcount growing or shrinking over time?
   -> Headcount trend line
3. Which departments are the biggest, and how does each contribute?
   -> Department breakdown chart
4. In which kind of location do our people work?
   -> Location tier chart
5. How do people work: office, hybrid or remote?
   -> Work mode chart

   ## Colours
- Blue: headcount (the main measure)
- Orange: the group the chart title is about
- Grey: all other groups
