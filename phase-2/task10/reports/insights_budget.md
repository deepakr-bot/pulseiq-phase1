# Task 10: Budget vs actual and KPI targets

## Sign rule
Variance = Actual − Budget. A positive variance means overspend (shown in red). A negative variance means underspend (shown in green). Variance % = Variance ÷ Budget × 100.

## Headline
Across all months (2025-04 to 2026-09), total payroll is ₹85.28 Cr against a budget of ₹85.31 Cr, a variance of −₹3.50 L (−0.04%). Total payroll is almost on budget, but 7 of 10 departments are over budget. The total hides the department-level overspend, so decisions should be made per department.

## Ranking (biggest overspend first, full period)
1. Data & AI: +₹9.93 L (+0.90%), over budget in 10 of 18 months
2. Product & Design: +₹6.08 L (+0.87%), over budget in 12 of 18 months
3. Customer Support: +₹4.83 L (+1.01%), over budget in 12 of 18 months

Engineering is the largest underspender at −₹20.55 L (−0.60%).

## Hiring freeze (freeze month 2025-07)
| Department | Before freeze | From 2025-07 | Post-freeze overspend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data & AI | −0.25% | +1.15% | ₹10.40 L (about ₹69,337 a month) |
| Product & Design | −0.57% | +1.16% | ₹6.75 L (about ₹45,015 a month) |

Both departments ran slightly under budget before the freeze and moved above budget after it. The budget was not updated after the freeze, so the post-freeze figure compares actual payroll with an outdated plan. Actual payroll is above even that plan, so the overspend looks real.

## KPI traffic lights (2026-09)
32 Green, 5 Amber, 2 Red, 1 Grey. Grey is "no target" for Task completion in Legal & Compliance. Two Red entries are Finance & Accounts (Attendance rate) and Sales (Monthly attrition).

## Recommendations
- **Data & AI:** Review the ₹10.40 L post-freeze overspend (about ₹69,337 a month) against pay rates and open roles, and reset the H2 budget with finance by 15 Dec 2026.
- **Product & Design:** Hold backfills for open roles until the revised budget is approved, and review the ₹6.75 L post-freeze overspend (about ₹45,015 a month) with finance by 15 Dec 2026.
